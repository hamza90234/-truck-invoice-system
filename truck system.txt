Truck Repair Shop Management System — Complete User Flow
 Main Users
For the current version, there are mainly two sides:
1. Shop Owner
The owner uses the system to:
•	Manage customers 
•	Manage trucks/vehicles 
•	Create repair invoices 
•	Manage parts and inventory 
•	Manage vendors 
•	Receive payments 
•	Send invoices 
•	Track expenses 
•	Reconcile bank transactions 
•	View profit and reports 
2. Customer
The customer doesn't necessarily need an account in the app.
They receive an invoice link and can:
•	View the invoice 
•	See balance 
•	View payment history 
•	Make payment 
•	Download invoice/receipt 
•	Approve an estimate when applicable 

EVENT 1 : Owner Creates His Shop Profile
User Story
As a shop owner, I want to create my shop profile so that my business information automatically appears on invoices and customer communications.
Owner enters:
•	Shop name 
•	Business address 
•	Phone number 
•	Email 
•	Website 
•	Logo 
•	Tax information 
•	Default tax rate 
•	Default payment terms 
•	Credit-card processing fee 
•	Invoice settings 
Example:
ABC Truck Repair

123 Main Street
Houston, TX

Phone: 555-1234
Email: abc@truckrepair.com

Default Tax: 8.25%
Credit Card Fee: 3%
________________________________________
EVENT 2 — Owner Logs Into the System
User Story
As the owner, I want to securely log in so that only authorized people can access my business information.
Owner:
Email
Password
      ↓
Login
      ↓
Dashboard
________________________________________
EVENT 3 — Owner Creates a Customer
A truck arrives at the shop.
The owner needs to record who owns the truck.
User Story
As the owner, I want to create a customer record so that I can keep their contact, billing, and business information.
Owner enters:
•	Company 
•	Contact person 
•	Phone 
•	Email 
•	Billing address 
•	Service address 
•	USDOT 
•	MC number 
•	EIN 
•	Customer type 
•	Payment terms 
•	Notes 
Example:
ABC Trucking LLC
John Smith
555-1234
john@abctrucking.com
Payment Terms:
Net 30
Customer Type:
Fleet
________________________________________
EVENT 4 — Owner Adds Customer's Truck
Now the owner needs to know which vehicle is being repaired.
User Story
As the owner, I want to add the customer's vehicle so that repair work can be connected to a specific truck.
Owner enters:
•	Unit number 
•	VIN 
•	Year 
•	Make 
•	Model 
•	Engine 
•	Transmission 
•	License plate 
•	Mileage 
•	Equipment type 
The owner can:
Scan VIN
or enter it manually.
The system:
•	Validates VIN 
•	Checks whether VIN already exists 
•	Warns about duplicates 

EVENT 5 — Customer Brings Truck for Repair
Now the actual repair job begins.
Example:
ABC Trucking brings Unit #101 because the brakes need repair.
Owner searches:
ABC Trucking
      ↓
Unit #101
      ↓
Create Repair Invoice
________________________________________
EVENT 6 — Owner Creates Invoice
User Story
As the owner, I want to create an invoice for the customer's repair so that I can record the work and charge the customer.
Invoice gets a unique number:
Invoice #INV-1001
It contains:
•	Customer 
•	Vehicle 
•	Date 
•	Due date 
•	Payment terms 
________________________________________
EVENT 7 — Owner Adds Labor
Owner records the work performed.
Example:
Brake Repair

Labor:
4 hours

Rate:
$100/hour

Total:
$400
Optional:
•	Technician 
•	Notes 
•	Warranty information 
System automatically calculates:
4 × $100 = $400
________________________________________
EVENT 8 — Owner Adds Parts
Owner searches inventory.
Example:
Brake Pad
Part #: BP-100
System shows:
Purchase Cost: $80
Selling Price: $120
Stock: 10
Owner adds:
Quantity: 2
System calculates:
2 × $120 = $240
________________________________________
EVENT 9 — Inventory Updates
When the invoice is completed:
Before:
Brake Pads = 10

Used:
2

After:
Brake Pads = 8
The system automatically deducts the parts.
If stock gets low:
⚠️ Low Inventory
________________________________________
EVENT 10 — Invoice Calculates Total
The system calculates:
Labor             $400
Parts             $240
-----------------------
Subtotal          $640
Tax                $XX
-----------------------
Invoice Total     $XXX
Owner doesn't manually calculate anything.
________________________________________
EVENT 11 — Owner Reviews Invoice
Before closing/sending:
Owner checks:
•	Customer 
•	Vehicle 
•	Labor 
•	Parts 
•	Tax 
•	Total 
•	Payment terms 
•	Warranty/return messages 
Then:
Preview Invoice
________________________________________
EVENT 12 — Owner Sends Invoice
User Story
As the owner, I want to send the invoice to the customer so that the customer can review and pay it.
Owner can send:
•	Email 
•	Text 
•	Online invoice link 
•	PDF 
•	PDF + link 
Example message:
Your invoice #INV-1001 is ready. Current balance is $640. You can review and pay using the link below.
________________________________________
EVENT 13 — Customer Opens Invoice
Customer receives the link.
They see:
ABC Truck Repair

Invoice #INV-1001

Vehicle:
Unit #101

Labor          $400
Parts          $240

Total          $640
Paid             $0
Balance        $640

[ PAY NOW ]
The customer doesn't need a full app account.
________________________________________
EVENT 14 — Customer Makes Payment
Customer chooses a payment method.
Possible methods:
•	Credit card 
•	Debit card 
•	ACH 
•	Zelle 
•	Cash 
•	Check 
•	EFS/Comchek 
•	Other 
For online card payment, a payment processor would actually process the card transaction.
________________________________________
EVENT 15 — Split Payment
Customer could pay:
Cash          $200
Credit Card   $300
Zelle         $140
-------------------
Total Paid    $640
System records each payment separately.
________________________________________
EVENT 16 — Credit Card Fee
If credit card payment is $300:
Credit Card Payment = $300

Fee = 3%

Fee = $9
The system adds the applicable fee according to the shop's settings.
________________________________________
EVENT 17 — Payment Status Updates
Partial payment
If customer pays $300:
Invoice Total       $640
Paid                $300
Remaining           $340

Status:
PARTIALLY PAID
Full payment
Customer later pays $340:
Invoice Total       $640
Paid                $640
Remaining             $0

Status:
PAID
The same invoice link now shows:
PAID

Balance: $0.00
________________________________________
EVENT 18 — Invoice Is Closed
User Story
As the owner, I want to close an invoice so that completed invoices cannot accidentally be changed.
When closed:
•	Invoice becomes locked 
•	Payment history is saved 
•	Customer balance updates 
•	Inventory updates 
•	Financial reports update 
•	Closing user is recorded 
•	Date/time is recorded 
If changes are required later:
Manager/Owner authorization required.
________________________________________
EVENT 19 — Customer Receives Paid Receipt
After payment, owner can send:
•	Paid invoice 
•	Receipt 
•	Updated PDF 
•	Payment confirmation 
The PDF shows:
Invoice Total: $640
Amount Paid:   $640
Balance:         $0

STATUS: PAID
________________________________________
